Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting the privacy of every patient, caregiver, and visitor who entrusts HealthTech with their health information and digital experience.

  1. Home
  2. Privacy

Last updated: October 1, 2025

Information We Collect

HealthTech collects only the information required to coordinate care, deliver our digital services, and comply with healthcare regulations. We collect information directly from you, from your providers, and from the devices you use to access our services.

Personal Information

  • Identity details such as name, date of birth, and preferred contact information
  • Medical history, diagnoses, medications, allergies, and care team notes
  • Insurance, billing, and payment details needed to process services
  • Account credentials and security questions used to protect your profile

Usage Information

We collect device identifiers, browser type, IP address, and interaction history to help us secure our systems, troubleshoot issues, and better understand how our digital tools are used.

How We Use Your Information

Your information enables us to coordinate care, deliver personalized services, and improve the HealthTech experience. We never use your health data for decisions unrelated to your care without your consent.

Primary Uses

  1. Scheduling appointments, managing referrals, and routing secure messages
  2. Providing clinicians with accurate and timely information about your health
  3. Supporting digital services such as telehealth visits, reminders, and patient education
  4. Meeting legal obligations, including reporting requirements and insurance audits

Information Sharing and Disclosure

We share information only with trusted parties who help us deliver and support your care. Every partner is bound by confidentiality agreements and required to protect your information.

We Never Sell Your Data

HealthTech does not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health information. We disclose information only with your authorization or when required by law.

Data Security

Protecting your data is a top priority. We employ administrative, technical, and physical safeguards aligned with HIPAA, SOC 2, and other industry frameworks.

Encryption

All health records, messages, and payment transactions are encrypted in transit and at rest.

Secure Infrastructure

We host data in certified data centers with continuous monitoring, redundancy, and access controls.

Your Rights

You have control over your information. Anytime you need to review, update, or limit how your data is used, our Privacy Office is ready to help.

Access and Copies

Request a digital or printed copy of your medical records and information we hold about you.

Corrections

Ask us to correct or complete information you believe is inaccurate or incomplete.

Deletion & Restrictions

Request that we delete certain information or restrict how it is used, subject to legal requirements.

Cookies and Tracking

HealthTech uses cookies and similar technologies to support secure sign-ins, remember your preferences, and measure site performance. You can adjust your browser settings to manage cookie usage at any time.

Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Essential Maintains secure login sessions and ensures core features function properly. Session
Analytics Helps us understand how our site is used so we can improve navigation and content. 13 Months
Preference Stores language, accessibility, and personalization choices you configure. 12 Months

Updates to This Policy

We review this policy regularly to reflect new services, regulatory changes, and technology advancements. When we make material updates, we will notify you through email or portal alerts.
