Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about scheduling, digital care, billing, and how HealthTech protects your information.

  2. FAQ

How do I schedule or change an appointment?

Use the HealthTech portal or call our CareLine at +1 (951) 684-9123. Same-day and virtual visits are released at 7:00 AM daily. If you need to reschedule, select the appointment in your portal dashboard or contact our scheduling team at least 24 hours in advance.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring a government-issued ID, insurance card, current medication list, and any recent labs or imaging. New patients can complete intake forms online through the portal so your care team can review them before you arrive.

Does HealthTech support telehealth visits?

Yes. Many primary care, follow-up, and specialty consults are available virtually. Log in to your account, choose "Virtual Visit," and ensure you have a strong internet connection and a private space. Your provider will join through our secure video platform.

How can I access my medical records or lab results?

All visit summaries and test results are posted to the HealthTech portal as soon as they are reviewed. You can download PDF copies or request printed records from our Health Information Management team by emailing records@healthtech.com.

What insurance plans do you accept?

We partner with most major commercial plans, Medicare, and select managed Medicaid networks. Visit our insurance page inside the portal or contact billing@healthtech.com with your plan details so our specialists can confirm coverage.

How does HealthTech protect my information?

We encrypt every record, require multifactor authentication for portal access, and monitor our systems 24/7. Only the clinicians involved in your care can access your data. Learn more in our Privacy Policy.
