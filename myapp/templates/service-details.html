Heart & Vascular Institute

Comprehensive Cardiology Program

Coordinated preventive, diagnostic, and interventional care for every stage of heart health.

Our board-certified cardiologists, cardiac surgeons, and rehabilitation specialists work as one integrated team. From proactive screenings to advanced minimally invasive procedures, we tailor every care plan around your goals and lifestyle.

Each patient is paired with a dedicated care navigator who coordinates appointments, ensures records flow securely between providers, and keeps you informed at every step.

Our Services Include:

  • Preventive risk assessments and lifestyle coaching
  • Advanced imaging including cardiac MRI, CT, and echo
  • Same-day diagnostic cath lab and electrophysiology studies
  • Structural heart and minimally invasive surgical procedures
  • 24/7 acute cardiac response and post-acute monitoring
  • Personalized recovery, rehabilitation, and remote monitoring programs
